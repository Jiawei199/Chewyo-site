Cookies
Cookies are small files that store bits of information on your computer or device when you visit the Site. They help us recognise your device and remember your preferences, such as your login details, language, or region, so you can browse more easily each time you return. Cookies also help us learn how visitors use the Site, like which pages are viewed most often or whether someone is a new or returning visitor.

We use several types of cookies to make sure the Site runs smoothly and offers a better experience for you:

  • Essential Cookies – keep the Site working as it should and enable basic functions.
  • Performance Cookies – help us understand how you use the Site so we can improve it.
  • Advertising Cookies – allow us to show you promotions and content that match your interests.
  • Social Media Cookies – make it easier to share content or connect through your social media accounts.


How long a cookie stays on your device depends on its type. Session cookies are temporary and are deleted once you close your browser, while persistent cookies stay until they expire or you remove them. Most of the cookies we use are persistent and typically last between 30 minutes and two years.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect how the Site functions and limit some features. Most browsers accept cookies automatically, but you can change this in your browser’s “Tools” or “Preferences” menu. For more information, visit www.allaboutcookies.org.

Even if you block cookies, some information may still be shared with trusted third parties such as advertising partners. To manage how your data is used for advertising, please see the Behavioural Advertising section above.
